# Tinytide Privacy Policy / 摇摇隐私政策

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

---

## English

Tinytide is designed so that **your family's data stays with you**.

**What we collect:** nothing. Tinytide has no servers, no accounts, no analytics,
no advertising and no tracking. The developer cannot see anything you log.

**Where your data is stored**
- On your device, and in **your own private iCloud database** (Apple CloudKit),
  if you are signed in to iCloud. This is how Tinytide backs up your data and syncs
  it between your devices. Apple handles this storage under
  [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- Widgets and Live Activities read a small summary stored on your device.

**Sharing with family:** if you choose to share a baby with someone, Apple's
iCloud sharing makes that baby's entries available to the people you invite.
You can see who has access and stop sharing at any time in
Tinytide's **Settings → Family & Caregivers**.

**Exports and backups:** backup and CSV files you export are created only when
you ask, and go only where you send them. Tinytide also keeps up to 10
automatic daily backups in its own storage on your iPhone, so mistakes can be
undone. They never leave your device, except as part of your iPhone's own
iCloud or computer backup, and you can delete them in
Tinytide's **Settings → Automatic Backups**.

**Notifications:** reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

**Purchases:** the optional Tinytide Supporter subscription and tips are
processed entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. Tinytide never receives
your payment details.

**Children's privacy:** Tinytide is used by parents and caregivers to record
information about their children. It does not collect any of that information.

**Deleting your data:** delete a baby in Tinytide's **Settings → Babies**, or delete
the app.
To remove the iCloud copy, go to **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage
Account Storage → Tinytide** on your iPhone.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be sent to
[support@tinytide.app](mailto:support@tinytide.app).

## 简体中文

摇摇（Tinytide）的设计原则是：**你家的数据只属于你**。

**我们收集什么数据：** 不收集任何数据。摇摇没有服务器、不需要注册账号，没有数据分析，没有广告，也没有任何追踪。开发者看不到你记录的任何内容。

**数据存放在哪里**
- 存放在你的设备上；如果登录了 iCloud，也会存放在**你自己的私有 iCloud 数据库**（Apple CloudKit）中，用于备份和在你的设备之间同步。这部分存储由 Apple 依照 [Apple 隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) 提供。
- 小组件和实时活动读取的是保存在你设备上的一份简要摘要。

**与家人共享：** 如果你选择共享某个宝宝，Apple 的 iCloud 共享会让你邀请的人看到该宝宝的记录。你可以随时在 **更多 → 家人与看护人** 中查看谁有访问权限，或停止共享。

**导出与备份：** 你导出的备份文件和 CSV 文件只在你主动导出时生成，并且只会发送到你选择的地方。摇摇还会在 iPhone 上 App 自己的存储空间里保留最多 10 份每日自动备份，方便撤销误操作。这些备份不会离开你的设备（iPhone 自身的 iCloud 或电脑备份除外），你可以在 **更多 → 自动备份** 中删除它们。

**通知：** 提醒都在你的设备上本地生成和安排。

**购买：** 可选的“摇摇支持者”订阅和打赏全程由 Apple 通过 App 内购买处理，摇摇不会接触到你的任何支付信息。

**儿童隐私：** 摇摇供家长和看护人记录孩子的情况，但不会收集任何这些信息。

**删除数据：** 在 **更多 → 宝宝** 中删除宝宝，或直接删除 App。如需删除 iCloud 中的数据，请在 iPhone 上前往 **设置 → [你的名字] → iCloud → 管理账户储存空间 → 摇摇**。

**联系方式：** 如对本政策有疑问，请发邮件到 [support@tinytide.app](mailto:support@tinytide.app)。
