# Third-Party Notices

Tinytide includes or is built with the following third-party material.

## WHO Child Growth Standards

`Packages/BabyCore/Sources/BabyCore/GrowthStandardsData.swift` contains the LMS
parameters (birth to 24 months) for weight-for-age, length-for-age and head
circumference-for-age from the **WHO Child Growth Standards**, as republished by
the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention:

- World Health Organization. *WHO Child Growth Standards: Methods and development.* Geneva: WHO, 2006. https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards
- CDC, *WHO Growth Chart data files*: https://www.cdc.gov/growthcharts/who-data-files.htm

The app displays the attribution "Growth charts use the WHO Child Growth Standards."
