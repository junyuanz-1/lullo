Tinytide is a gentle baby tracker for sleep, feeds, diapers and growth. One tap to log, easy to read at 3 a.m., and shared in real time with everyone who helps.

ONE-TAP LOGGING
• Start sleep, nursing and pumping timers with a single tap, and stop them from the Lock Screen or the Dynamic Island
• Left and right nursing timer that remembers which side is next
• Bottles with amount and contents, pumping per side, and solids with reactions
• Diapers with pee amount, poop color and consistency, and diaper rash
• Medication with dose reminders, temperature, symptoms, vaccines, activities like tummy time and bath, plus notes and photos
• Made a mistake? Undo it right away

NAP SWEET SPOT
• See when the next nap is likely to go well, based on your baby's age and their own recent wake windows

TRENDS AND GROWTH
• A 24-hour daily rhythm chart, and 7, 14 and 30-day trends for sleep, feeds, diapers and pumping
• Weight, length and head circumference with WHO growth percentiles (0 to 24 months)

SHARED WITH FAMILY
• Invite a partner, grandparent or nanny with their own Apple Account. Everyone logs and sees the same day, in real time
• Choose who can edit and who can only view. Every entry shows who logged it

GENTLE ALARMS
• Set a countdown when you start a timer, like 10 minutes per side, with soft sounds such as a music box or wind chimes

ALWAYS AT HAND
• Live Activities, plus Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
• Siri: "Baby is sleeping in Tinytide" or "Baby woke up in Tinytide"
• Available in 9 languages

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything is stored on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud
• Automatic daily backups on your iPhone, plus backup files and CSV export to move to a new phone or share with your pediatrician

FREE, WITH AN OPTIONAL SUPPORTER SUBSCRIPTION
Every tracking feature is free. Tinytide Supporter is an optional auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription that unlocks alternate app icons, theme colors and a supporter badge, as a thank-you for supporting an independent app. You can also leave a one-time tip.

Tinytide is not a medical device. Nap suggestions, growth percentiles and health tips are general information. Always talk to your pediatrician about your baby's health.
